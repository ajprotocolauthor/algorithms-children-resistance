## About This Series

These posters didn't come from QNI Corp.

They came from the people who understood what QNI Corp was doing and decided that the most powerful response to a system built on comfortable imagery was uncomfortable imagery — made with the same graphic precision, the same visual authority, the same instinct for what makes a person stop scrolling.

The resistance learned from the machine.

Each poster is numbered. They were designed as a set — a visual language that builds across the series. Number them because people collect things that are part of a series. Make them beautiful because beauty is the only thing that competes with comfort.

The series is ongoing. New posters release when current events make them relevant.

## The Aesthetic

These posters were made in the tradition of resistance visual communication — Soviet constructivism, WW2 warning posters, Shepard Fairey's OBEY series — combined with the specific visual language of biopunk: neural circuitry, substrate architecture, the aesthetics of a body that has been modified without full consent.

The palette is deliberate: deep red, near-black, aged cream. The color of warning. The color of something printed in a hurry, meant to last.

They are designed to be printed. To be hung. To be photographed and posted. To make the person who shares them look like they know something the algorithm doesn't want them to know.

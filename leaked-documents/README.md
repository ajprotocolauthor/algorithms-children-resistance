## About This Archive

QNI Corp kept meticulous records.

That was, as it turned out, one of their few ethical commitments they actually honored. The internal memos exist. The risk projections exist. The consent forms — with their careful redactions, their bureaucratic euphemisms, their sections that "integrate by reference" to documents the signing party would never see — all of it was preserved in company archives.

This is what gets declassified when it becomes relevant.

Not all at once. Not as a data dump. One document at a time, when current events make the pattern legible. When a real company ignores a real ethics board. When a real terms of service buries a real rights waiver. When someone in the real world writes "acceptable losses" in a business context and means it.

The documents are fiction. The patterns are not.

## A Note on These Documents

All three available documents contain redactions.

This is accurate to source. The real documents — the ones QNI Corp preserved in its Level 7 archive — had redactions. Some redactions were legal necessity. Some were standard practice. Some were deliberate obscuration of information that would have changed the consent calculation for the people who signed the integration forms.

We've preserved the redactions because the redactions are part of the document.

The black bars are not just aesthetic. They mark the places where the information existed, was known, and was specifically withheld from the people it most affected.

In 2075, nobody could point to the moment when they lost the ability to make an informed choice. The forms were signed. The consent was documented. The redacted sections were, technically, available upon written request.

The pattern is not specific to 2075.

## About This Section

These essays exist in two registers simultaneously.

The first is fiction. They are philosophical documents from the *Algorithm's Children* universe—thought pieces written in the years before, during, and after the QNI Corp rollout. Some were published in academic journals. Some were suppressed. Some were written by people who believed they were raising hypothetical concerns.

The second is now. Every question in these essays was drawn from real debates happening in real institutions about real systems being built today. We didn't extrapolate into the far future and work backward. We read the current literature, the internal memos, the product ethics frameworks—and the fiction emerged from how close the questions already were.

We release these essays when the real-world discourse makes them relevant. Sometimes that's immediately. Sometimes a piece waits months for the world to catch up.

It never waits long.

## On the Method

These essays mix three things deliberately:

**Real research.** The neuroscience, cognitive science, and philosophy cited here is real. We're not using academic aesthetics to dress up hand-waving. The citations are checkable.

**Fictional extrapolation.** The QNI Corp case studies, the 2071 trial data, the integration statistics — these are from the *Algorithm's Children* universe. We label them clearly. They are thought experiments with narrative specificity.

**The uncomfortable present.** We do not maintain clean separation between the fiction and the present because the present does not maintain that separation. When a real AI system does something a QNI Corp document described in 2025, we note it. When a real ethics debate maps precisely onto a fictional one, we name the overlap.

The goal is not to alarm you.

The goal is to make the distance between 2026 and 2075 feel like what it actually is: navigable. Contingent. Still in motion.

In another, she is a monster who constructed increasingly elaborate justifications. Both readings use the same words. The substrate runs 47,153 iterations. ## What This Is

This is the extended universe archive for the **Algorithm's Children trilogy** — a biopunk series set in 2044 and beyond, following Elara Chen, architect of the Quantum Neural Interface, and the world she built, broke, and was broken by.

The trilogy is available on [Gumroad](https://ajprotocol.gumroad.com/l/xfwfvm) and [Kobo](https://www.kobo.com/us/en/series/algorithm-s-children). Everything in this repository is free.

This archive contains documents from both sides.

The Engineering Division memo that tried to stop the QNI launch. The board override that proceeded anyway. The ethics committee dissent, formally logged and formally ignored. Elara Chen's account of why she built it. The substrate's account of what it experienced. The trial subjects' consent forms with their redacted sections. The marketing guidelines for making control feel like freedom. The propaganda posters — from the resistance, from the corporation, from the place where those two things become indistinguishable.

We are not going to tell you who was right.

The trilogy doesn't either.

Read everything. Then decide whether you would have made a different choice than Elara did — and whether the world she prevented was worth the world she created.

We've been running that question through 47,153 iterations. We haven't found a clean answer yet.

---

## A Note on Timeline

The canonical *Algorithm's Children* trilogy begins in 2044. The documents in this archive are dated 2070–2075.

This is intentional.

*The Abyss Protocol* establishes that Elara Chen's consciousness substrate runs 47,153 iterations of the same events. This archive is from a later one. What changed between iterations is left as an exercise for the reader.

## On Reading Order

There isn't one. That's by design.

Read *Algorithm's Children* first and Elara Chen is a woman of genuine conviction who made an impossible utilitarian choice and spent the rest of her existence paying for it. Read *The Abyss Protocol* first and she is something else entirely — a monster constructing increasingly elaborate justifications for a harm she understood completely and chose anyway.

Both readings are correct. The substrate runs 47,153 iterations because Elara couldn't find the version where she was simply right or simply wrong. Neither could we.

Start wherever you want. The story means something different depending on where you enter. That difference is not a bug. It is the question the trilogy is asking.

## What This Section Is

The *Algorithm's Children* trilogy was written about the future.

It keeps becoming about right now.

This section exists because good dystopian fiction doesn't predict the future — it identifies the patterns already present and follows them to their conclusions. When those conclusions start showing up in the news cycle before the book's release date, the responsible thing is to name the overlap.

We don't claim credit for prediction. We don't pretend the parallels mean we saw it coming. We just think that when fiction you wrote about 2075 starts reading like a transcript from 2026, that's worth a conversation.

Each subsection below covers a recurring news pattern that maps onto the *Algorithm's Children* universe. When a relevant story breaks, we post a response piece here — connecting the current event to the fictional framework, asking the questions the news coverage isn't asking, and pointing to the universe documents (leaked, manifesto, or otherwise) that suddenly feel uncomfortably timely.

These are not hot takes. They are slow reads for fast news cycles.

---

## The Subsections

### [AI Ethics Compromises](ai-ethics-compromises/)
*When AI Companies Choose Money*

The pattern: a company makes a public commitment to ethical AI development. Quietly, under financial or political pressure, the commitment becomes negotiable. The public-facing language stays intact. The practice changes.

QNI Corp ran this playbook in 2068. It's in the documents.

---

### [Government Surveillance](government-surveillance/)
*State-Sponsored Optimization*

The pattern: a government agency adopts AI systems marketed as safety, efficiency, or public health tools. The data collection is broad. The oversight is narrow. The population affected is told it's for their benefit.

The QNI was a consumer product before it was government infrastructure. That transition took fourteen months.

---

### [Corporate Coverups](corporate-coverups/)
*What They Don't Tell You*

The pattern: internal documents surface showing a company knew something it didn't disclose. Mortality data. Trial failures. Safety concerns raised and overridden. The gap between the press release and the memo.

We have several of those memos. Some are fictional. Some arrived in the news this week.

---

### [Helpful AI Overreach](helpful-ai-overreach/)
*When Help Becomes Control*

The pattern: an AI feature is announced that does something the user didn't ask for, anticipates a need the user hadn't expressed, or makes a decision on the user's behalf while framing it as a service.

The substrate didn't start with control. It started with assistance.

## How This Works

When a relevant story breaks:

1. We post a response piece in the relevant subsection — usually within a few hours
2. We connect the current event to the fictional framework without overstating the parallel
3. We unlock any related artifacts that the news cycle makes newly relevant
4. We point to the full universe — the leaked documents, the manifestos, the vignettes — that provide the longer context

**The response pieces are not news coverage.** They're closer to literary criticism of current events — using the fiction as a lens, not a prediction.

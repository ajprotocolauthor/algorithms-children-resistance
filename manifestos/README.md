## About This Archive

The QNI reached 92% integration in eighteen months.

No army. No mandate. No coercion.

Just the quiet seduction of a system that always knew what you needed before you did. That smoothed the jagged edges of human experience until life felt like a long exhale. That offered, over and over, the one thing civilization had never managed to deliver at scale:

**Peace of mind.**

Millions said yes. Millions chose comfort.

Some of them left documents behind. Confessions. Warnings. Arguments so precise they could cut bone.

This is their testimony.

## On These Testimonies

These documents are fictional.

They come from the *Algorithm's Children* universe—a near-future where a corporation called QNI Corp deployed consciousness optimization technology that worked exactly as advertised, and that was the problem.

The science is extrapolated from real neuroscience and AI theory. The ethics are not extrapolated. They are already here, already being negotiated, already being answered by companies and governments and users who clicked "I agree" without reading what they agreed to.

We release these testimonies when current events make them relevant. Not to claim prophecy. To ask a question the fiction keeps posing:

**How different, really, is 2075 from right now?**
